Dear Esteemed Alumni,

ECE Department • Alumni Relations Cell

It is with great pride that I write to you as Dean, Alumni Relations, on behalf of the Sanjivani College of Engineering, Kopargaon. Sanjivani's journey began with a clear purpose: to bring world-class engineering education to the rural heartland of Kopargaon.

This vision, set in motion by our Founder, Late Shri Shankarrao Genuji Kolhe, has since been carried forward with unwavering commitment by our Executive Trustee, Hon. Shri Nitindada Kolhe, and our Managing Trustee, Hon. Shri Amitdada Kolhe. Together, their leadership has transformed that founding vision into a thriving global community of 35,000+ alumni, spread across 30+ countries.

Our alumni remain central to this institution's continued growth. Your professional achievements enhance Sanjivani's reputation, your insights help us keep our curriculum industry-relevant, and your engagement with current students provides guidance no classroom alone can offer. I encourage each of you to stay actively involved with your alma mater through:

Mentorship
Guest Lectures, Internships and Placements
Scholarships and Infrastructure Support
Simply strengthening your connection with the institute

We look forward to your continued partnership as Sanjivani builds on its legacy for the generations ahead.

With warm regards and Sanjivani pride,
Dr. N. K. Darwante

Reconnecting Across Continents

Flagship Alumni Meets AY 2025–26

This year, the Student Alumni Relation Cell (SARC) took the Sanjivani spirit across three continents — bringing together alumni for an evening of memories, mentorship pledges and renewed pride in their alma mater.

USA & Canada Chapter Reunion — “Westward Ho!”

23 August 2025  |  Las Vegas, USA  |  165 Alumni
24 August 2025  |  New Jersey, USA  |  37 Alumni

Sanjivani's first-ever USA & Canada Chapter reunion brought together 165 alumni for an evening of felicitations and heartfelt speeches from Hon. Amit Dada Kolhe (Managing Trustee), Dr. Shantam Shukla (Director, Strategic Planning) and Dr. A. G. Thakur (Vice Chancellor, Sanjivani University). The evening closed with the launch of the USA & Canada Chapter and a promise of more global chapters to come.

UK & Ireland Alumni Meet

2 November 2025  |  The Indian Gymkhana Club, Isleworth, UK  |  ~40 Alumni

Organized in collaboration with the Sanjivani Alumni Association of UK & Ireland, this grand first-ever London reunion welcomed alumni working across the UK and Ireland, graced by the presence of Hon. Amitdada Kolhe, Managing Trustee, Sanjivani Group of Institutes. Alumni spanning the 1991 to 2024 batches reconnected, shared their global career journeys, and pledged continued mentorship support for current students.

Gujarat Chapter Alumni Reunion

25 January 2026  |  Vadodara, Gujarat  |  53 Alumni

Bringing together 15 alumni of Sanjivani College of Engineering and 38 alumni of Sanjivani College of Pharmaceutical Education and Research, the Gujarat Chapter reunion focused on curriculum feedback, industry trends and renewed commitments to guest lectures, internships and mentorship for current students.

SRESCOE 2009 Batch Alumni Reunion — 2026

7–8 February 2026  |  Batch of 2009

Alumni of the 2009 batch of Sanjivani College of Engineering came together for a dedicated batch reunion, reconnecting with classmates and sharing their professional journeys since graduation. The gathering reinforced strong peer bonds and renewed enthusiasm for continued engagement with the institute, with alumni pledging support through mentorship, curriculum feedback and industry collaboration.

Looking Ahead

Our Vision for AY 2026–27

  • • Complete alumni registration and verification for every graduating batch, with authenticated records maintained on the digital alumni portal.
  • • Sustain regular alumni engagement through department-level meets, national chapter gatherings, and city-wise reunions across India and abroad.
  • • Strengthen the mentorship ecosystem, connecting every student with an alumni mentor through guest sessions, storyboards, and industry interactions.
  • • Deepen alumni-driven career support, from internships and placement referrals to participation in curriculum reviews and Boards of Studies.
  • • Keep the community connected through newsletters, social media, and continued outreach that celebrates alumni achievements.
  • • Grow resource mobilization and giving back, through alumni-funded scholarships, infrastructure support, and departmental initiatives.
