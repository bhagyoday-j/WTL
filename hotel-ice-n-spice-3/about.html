About Us

Our Story

Hotel Ice N Spice is a popular family restaurant located at Yeola Naka, Shardanagar, on the Shirdi-Manmad Highway. We are committed to serving delicious food and creating memorable dining experiences.

Our menu includes a variety of Indian dishes, refreshing beverages and special family meals.
